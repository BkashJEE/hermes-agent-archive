ElevenLabs Music

By Nous Research · Official Docs · Date unknown

ElevenLabs Music for Hermes: describe a song or paste your lyrics and Hermes writes, generates and sends the track, 3 seconds to 10 minutes, vocals or instrumental. It can redo one section, extend or loop a song, split it into stems (vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano, other) and score a video. Long songs finish in the background and arrive in the chat. Bundles a music skill (prompting, composition plans, edit recipes) and, in Hermes Desktop, an inline music card with synced lyrics, format downloads and a music library tab. Model, audio format and endpoint are plugin settings. Disclosure: uses your ElevenLabs API key (ELEVENLABS_API_KEY, the same key as Hermes's ElevenLabs voice; needs a paid ElevenLabs plan, and a restricted key needs the Music permission); compose, plan, upload, stems and video calls are paid generations billed to your ElevenLabs account by length (drafting a plan and listing finetunes are free). Only the prompt or plan, its parameters and any file you name are sent, to api.elevenlabs.io (or the data-residency endpoint you set); with store_for_editing on, ElevenLabs keeps generated songs so they can be edited later. Files are written to cache/generated/audio under the Hermes home and never overwritten. One Python dependency (httpx, already part of Hermes).

Requires Hermes >=0.22.0. Tools: elevenlabs_music_compose, elevenlabs_music_compose_plan, elevenlabs_music_draft_plan, elevenlabs_music_upload, elevenlabs_music_stems, elevenlabs_music_from_video, elevenlabs_music_finetunes, elevenlabs_music_job. Environment: ELEVENLABS_API_KEY. Version 1.0.0 at import.

hermes plugins install elevenlabs-music
