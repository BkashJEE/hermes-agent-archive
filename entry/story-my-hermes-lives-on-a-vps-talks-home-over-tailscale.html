My Hermes lives on a VPS, talks home over Tailscale

By @sammcf · Discord · April 6, 2026

the agent should always run in /yolo mode or equivalent. it's up to you as the human to make sure it is incapable of breaking anything - my hermes lives on a vps, for example, and it can only talk to my pc at home via tailscale, and then only through a scoped tag. they really excel at long-term tasks where you don't want to be giving them feedback regularly!
