I now compile skills into code, only invoking AI at necessary steps

By @jerometao_38874 · Discord

I once built skills that worked very well with advanced models connected to the Hermes Agent, but they performed poorly when switched to weaker models. For this reason, I now prefer compiling skills into code, only involving AI at necessary steps. This greatly improves the reliability and consistency of execution.
