Teams Pipeline

By Nous Research · Official Docs · Date unknown

Microsoft Teams meeting pipeline: turns Microsoft Graph change notifications from the msgraph_webhook gateway platform into durable jobs that fetch the meeting transcript (or the recording, transcribed), summarize it with your auxiliary model and deliver the summary to Teams, plus `hermes teams-pipeline` to list, inspect and re-run jobs, check Graph setup and maintain subscriptions. Notion and Linear writers exist but are not wired yet, and a re-run re-summarizes without delivering. Tested with stand-ins only, not against a real Microsoft 365 tenant. Formerly built into Hermes core; existing users are migrated automatically. Disclosure: one Python dependency (httpx). It calls Microsoft Graph (login.microsoftonline.com, graph.microsoft.com) with the app-only credentials in MSGRAPH_TENANT_ID / MSGRAPH_CLIENT_ID / MSGRAPH_CLIENT_SECRET, reading the meeting artifacts your app registration is granted. Transcripts go to your configured auxiliary model (and a recording to your transcription provider) to be summarized; summaries go only to the Teams destination you configure. Job state, including the Graph subscription clientState, is kept in teams_pipeline_store.json in your profile.

Requires Hermes >=0.21.7. Environment: MSGRAPH_TENANT_ID, MSGRAPH_CLIENT_ID, MSGRAPH_CLIENT_SECRET. Version 0.2.0 at import.
