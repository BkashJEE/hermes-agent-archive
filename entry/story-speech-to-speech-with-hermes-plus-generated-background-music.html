Speech-to-speech with Hermes, plus generated background music

By @.s0uthpaw · Discord

I work with Hermes almost exclusively speech-to-speech until my kids go to bed and I can sit down and focus at my desk. Well I added a little skill to my Hermes that plays generated background music while it is working and fades out when Hermes stops. It's nicer than listening for my GPUs to stop spinning lol and if Hermes hits a wall, the music stops and I know from the other room.
