Voice-first fitness coach that learns my body over time

By @muschi2396 · Discord

Most people train hard but have no idea what's happening in their body so i built an AI agent that actually learns it over time. You tell it things like: 'leg day, sore in glutes not quads', 'ate x today', 'low energy' and it starts connecting the dots: training → nutrition → recovery → performance. It runs on telegram, voice-first, no tracking friction.
