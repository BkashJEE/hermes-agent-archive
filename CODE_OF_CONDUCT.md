# Community conduct

Be constructive and specific. Critique claims, code and curation decisions; do not harass people. Disagreement with the maintainer, including criticism of the star cutoff, is welcome when supported by a clear explanation.

No threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, impersonation, spam or unsolicited promotion. Do not post private conversations, credentials or personal information without permission. Disclose your connection when recommending your own project.

BkashJEE moderates issues and pull requests. Depending on the conduct, moderation may include requesting changes, hiding abusive material, closing a discussion or blocking an account. Decisions should explain the conduct involved where doing so does not expose private information.

For an ordinary dispute, reply constructively in the relevant issue. For a sensitive conduct report, open an issue requesting a private contact without including names or private evidence. Platform-level abuse can also be reported directly to GitHub. No response deadline is guaranteed.
