## Candidate: a personal Zalo account as a Hermes assistant

**Post author:** Cường Tuấn Nguyễn. **Original language:** Vietnamese.

[Original public Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2447817115671965/posts/2486058831847793/)

**English summary of the author's claims:** The plugin connects a personal Zalo
account to Hermes for direct and group conversations. Group replies can be limited
to mentions or replies, with allowed users/groups and separate permission levels.
The post describes QR sign-in and background operation.

**Limitation disclosed by the author:** It uses an unofficial Zalo API. Automated
accounts may be restricted or blocked. The author suggests a secondary account.
These capabilities were not execution-tested; no plugin was installed.

**Decision: research only.** The linked
[repository](https://github.com/cuongdev/hermes-zalo-plugin) had **42 stars** when
checked on this review date, below the strictly-more-than-50,000 rule. Source/credit:
[GitHub public repository API](https://api.github.com/repos/cuongdev/hermes-zalo-plugin),
field `stargazers_count`. Its API description explicitly identifies Hermes support,
but relevance does not override the star floor. Do not repackage this plugin as a
Facebook card to evade that rule. It is also not evidence of an undocumented trick.

## English-reader access

English text above is an editorial summary, not a verbatim quotation or an
author-approved translation. Preserve both creator and sharer credits.

Facebook's **See translation** control was tested on the Zalo post and produced an
English translation. It also produced English on the WordPress post, but rendered
the ambiguous security wording as "Scan Emails"; the tutorial needs checking to
resolve that discrepancy. Machine translation is not source verification. Readers may need
to sign in and select the control themselves; the permalink does not guarantee
English for every viewer. Do not append a locale parameter and claim that it
translates the post, or promise an untested translation-proxy link.
