# No Sitemap line on purpose. The archive is one page with hash routes (#skills,
# #skills?item=<id>), and a crawler discards everything after the '#', so every entry
# resolves to the same URL. A sitemap listing those fragments would claim 1,149 pages
# that do not exist as far as a crawler is concerned. Real per-entry URLs need
# pre-rendered pages; until those exist, naming a sitemap here would be a number nobody
# measured, which is the one thing this archive does not do.
